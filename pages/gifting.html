Gifting Guidelines

Bespoke Crate & Corporate Gifting FAQ

Common questions regarding customization lead times, volume logistics, and branding options.

What is the minimum lead time for custom corporate crate engraving?

For individual bespoke engraved orders (1–5 bottles), lead time is 48 to 72 hours plus courier transit. For corporate volume orders (20+ crates) requiring custom brass plaque milling or full case branding, we recommend allowing 7 to 10 business days. Expedited rush turnaround is available upon request.

Can corporate clients provide their own branded collateral or inserts?

Yes. Our packaging atelier seamlessly incorporates your corporate brochures, personalized executive letters on letterhead, bespoke ribbon colors, or engraved company pens inside each wooden crate alongside the sommelier documentation.

How do you guarantee wine integrity during international shipping?

All shipments use thermally insulated isothermal flight cases and temperature-monitoring indicators. During extreme summer heat or winter freeze, orders travel exclusively via cold-chain climate-controlled air transport to preserve bottle chemistry and label perfection.

Are corporate orders eligible for VAT deduction or bonded transfer?

Yes. Qualifying business entities receive VAT-compliant commercial invoices. In-bond transfers between HMRC/EU bonded warehouses can also be executed duty-suspended for corporate cellar accounts and international dispatch.
