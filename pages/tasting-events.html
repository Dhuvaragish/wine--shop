Connoisseur Inquiries

Masterclass & Tasting FAQ

Essential information for attending sommelier sessions at our Mayfair subterranean tasting salon.

How far in advance should tasting masterclasses be reserved?

Because our masterclasses are strictly capped at 8 connoisseurs to ensure conversational depth and ample uncorking portions, sessions typically book out 3 to 6 weeks in advance. Private cellar buyouts should be inquired at least 2 weeks prior to allow vintage decanting scheduling.

What is the dress code for cellar tastings and masterclasses?

We observe an elegant smart-casual to business-formal dress code. Because the tasting vault is subterranean and climate-regulated at an optimal 15°C (59°F) for vintage preservation, we recommend carrying a light blazer, wrap, or jacket for your comfort.

Can dietary restrictions or allergies be accommodated for the affineur pairings?

Yes, absolutely. Our culinary sommelier team accommodates vegetarian, gluten-free, dairy-free, and halal dietary requirements with bespoke artisanal alternatives when notified at least 48 hours in advance of your session.

Can I purchase the bottles sampled during the masterclass?

Yes. Masterclass guests enjoy priority cellar allocation for any featured vintages or whiskies on the evening of the event, with complimentary vault packing or climate-controlled white-glove courier dispatch to your residence.

What is the cancellation and ticket transfer policy?

Reservations may be rescheduled or cancelled with full credit to your Cellar Account up to 7 days prior to the event. Within 7 days, reservations may be freely transferred to a nominated guest by notifying our concierge.
