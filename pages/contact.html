Subterranean Sanctuary

The Mayfair Cellar Lounge Experience

Engineered beneath Grosvenor Square to recreate the historical maturation conditions of Saint-Émilion and Speyside, our lounge unites historic vault architecture with premier sommelier services.

The 12,000-Bottle Vault

Surrounded by centuries of liquid history, our subterranean repository maintains a strict 13°C (55°F) temperature and 70% humidity with low-UV amber illumination to ensure cellar-grade preservation.

Calacatta Coravin Bar

A hand-carved Italian marble bar seating 12 patrons. Sample unreleased First Growths and 50-year-old single malts poured by the glass via argon-needle preservation without extracting the cork.

Private Member Lockers

Heavy-gauge brass private lockers with biometric recognition and bonded cellar certification. Store personal trophy bottles in Mayfair with complimentary decanting and Riedel stemware service.

Sommelier Advisory Suite

One-on-one consultations with Master Sommeliers to architect residential cellars, acquire En Primeur allocations, authenticate vintage bottles, and structure tax-efficient wine investment portfolios.

Connoisseur Protocols

Cellar Lounge Visiting Guidelines

To maintain the pristine sensory environment required for vintage wine appreciation, we request that all guests observe our lounge etiquette.

Attire & Olfactory Courtesy

Dress code is smart elegant. To safeguard the delicate aromas of aged Grand Crus, we respectfully request that guests refrain from wearing heavy perfumes, colognes, or scented lotions.

Vault Climate & Cashmere

The subterranean vaults are maintained at 13°C (55°F) for optimal preservation. Complimentary Scottish cashmere stoles, jackets, and artisan espresso service are provided during cellar tours.

Reservations & Walk-ins

Our street-level Mayfair retail salon welcomes walk-in acquisitions during regular hours. Vault tastings, Coravin flights, and sommelier consultations require advance reservation.

Client Inquiries

Cellar Lounge & Tasting FAQs

Common questions regarding private room reservations, collection storage, and bonded shipping.

How far in advance should I reserve a private cellar tasting?

We recommend reserving 7 to 14 days in advance for weekend flights and evening sessions. For same-week availability or bespoke vintage flight requests, please contact our Direct Sommelier Line directly at +44 20 7946 0912.

What is the capacity for private dining or corporate celebrations in the Vault Salon?

The Subterranean Vault Salon accommodates up to 24 seated guests for sommelier-led multi-course wine dinners, or up to 45 guests for standing cocktail receptions. We partner with Michelin-starred culinary teams to orchestrate bespoke pairings customized to your selected vintages.

Can I purchase bottles for immediate takeaway or international bonded shipping?

Yes. All bottles in our Mayfair inventory are packaged in custom insulated wooden cases. We provide complimentary London courier delivery within 3 hours, as well as insured, climate-regulated airfreight to over 40 countries under duty-paid or bonded export status.

How does private member locker storage operate in Mayfair?

Lockers are reserved exclusively for our Connoisseur Circle members. Each locker holds between 24 and 72 bottles under monitored 13°C conditions. Members enjoy 24/7 biometric vault access, complimentary glassware prep, and sommelier decanting service whenever visiting the lounge.

Do you offer provenance appraisal and cellar consignment?

Yes. Our certified valuation team conducts detailed provenance audits, ullage checks, capsule verification, and market appraisals. We acquire exceptional private cellars directly or manage consignment placements through our international collector network.
